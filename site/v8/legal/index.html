Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information EternaWell Medicine ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the website only. Health information you share with us as a patient is also protected by HIPAA, as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

What we collect

  • Messages: your name, your email address or phone number, and the message you send us through the website.
  • Online booking: your name, phone number, email address, the visit type you choose and your appointment time. Booking records are deleted automatically after a set period once the visit has passed.
  • Calls and texts: your phone number and anything you tell us. Please don't send medical details by text or email.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, kept by our hosting provider for security and to keep the site running. [Confirm: no analytics or advertising trackers are used.]

How we use it

To reply to your messages, to schedule and confirm your visits, to answer your questions, and to keep the website secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the website, online booking and email, under contracts that limit how they can use it: Cloudflare, which hosts this website and stores booking records [add any others, e.g. an email provider]. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, call (206) 507-2504 or write to us at 2700 Richards Rd, Suite 204A, Bellevue, WA 98005.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This policy is provided under Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373). It applies to "consumer health data": personal information linked or reasonably linkable to you that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. Health information that is protected by HIPAA is covered by our Notice of Privacy Practices instead.

Consumer health data we collect

  • The visit type or service you choose when booking (for example, hormone replacement or prescription dermatology), which can suggest a health interest.
  • Any health information you choose to include in a message, or tell us by phone, text or email, before you become a patient.

Why we collect it

Only to schedule your visit, reply to your message, contact you about your visit, and prepare for your consultation. We collect it only with your consent, which you give when you book or send a message, and only as needed to provide the service you asked for.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, through our online booking and message forms, phone calls, texts or email.

Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We share it only with the processors that run our website and booking system (Cloudflare) [add any others], who may use it only on our behalf. We will not share it with anyone else without your separate consent.

Your rights

If you are a Washington resident, or your data is collected in Washington, you have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and see it, along with a list of the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with;
  • withdraw your consent to our collection and sharing of it;
  • have it deleted.

To make a request, call (206) 507-2504 or write to 2700 Richards Rd, Suite 204A, Bellevue, WA 98005. We will respond within 45 days, as the law requires. If we deny your request, you can appeal by contacting us the same way. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov.
