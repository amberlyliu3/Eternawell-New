Services

Care for every stage of life.

Four areas of care, each built on careful testing and a plan made for you. Every service is available in person in Bellevue, WA or by telemedicine.

FSA / HSA eligible · In person & telemedicine

Hormone replacement therapy

Bioidentical hormones · Lab-guided dosing · Regular monitoring

Hormone therapy tailored to your labs and symptoms, adjusted over time as your body changes.

  • Menopause Hot flashes, sleep disruption, low libido and mood changes.
  • Andropause Low energy, low libido and loss of strength from falling testosterone.
  • Adrenal fatigue Ongoing tiredness and stress intolerance, evaluated with a full hormone picture.
  • Fibromyalgia Widespread pain and unrefreshing sleep, with attention to hormonal contributors.
  • PCOS Cycle regulation, metabolic health and symptoms of polycystic ovary syndrome.

Weight management

Metabolic testing · Medication options · Ongoing support

Medically supervised weight management that looks at the hormonal and metabolic causes of weight gain, not just calories. Your plan may include lab work, prescription options where appropriate, and regular check-ins.
