Our care
What does "normal is not optimal" mean?

Standard lab reference ranges are built from population averages, which include a lot of people who don't feel their best. We look beyond whether your labs fall within a broad "normal" range and instead aim for the levels where you actually feel and function at your best.

What conditions do you treat?

We offer four areas of care: hormone replacement therapy (menopause, andropause, adrenal fatigue, fibromyalgia and PCOS), weight management, early coronary and cancer screening, and prescription dermatology (anti-aging, acne, skin brightening, rosacea, scars, warts, moles and hair loss).

Is bioidentical hormone therapy safe?

Bioidentical hormone therapy is individualized and monitored closely with regular lab work to keep your levels in a safe, optimal range. Your doctor will review your health history and any risk factors before starting treatment.

Membership & payment
Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes. Every new patient receives a complimentary 20-minute consultation to discuss your symptoms and goals. If you decide to move forward, the next step is a lab draw and full initial consultation to build your personalized plan.

Do I need to create an account to book?

No. You can book online with just your name, phone number and email. There is no account, no sign-up fee and no obligation. You only pay for the services you choose to receive, such as lab work or ongoing treatment.

Are your services FSA / HSA eligible?

Yes, FSA and HSA cards are accepted for eligible services and products, whether you're visiting in person or booking online.

Visits, booking & products
Do you offer telemedicine appointments?

Yes. Every service we offer is available either in person at our office or entirely by telemedicine. Labs can typically be drawn at a local facility near you if you choose the telemedicine option.

How do I book an appointment?

Pick a time on our booking page; no account is needed. Or call or text {{PHONE_DISPLAY}} if that's easier for you.

Can I purchase products without becoming a patient?

Our skin, hair, and hormone-support products are available to order online. For personalized supplement or treatment recommendations, we suggest booking a free consultation first.
